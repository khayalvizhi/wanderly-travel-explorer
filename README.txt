WANDERLY — TRAVEL EXPLORER
==========================

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the "wanderly" folder.
3. Double-click index.html, or open it with your browser.

Files:
- index.html  : Website structure and content
- style.css   : Responsive design and styling
- script.js   : Search, filters, favourites, mobile menu and budget calculator

Notes:
- Destination photos and Google Fonts are loaded from external URLs, so an internet connection is needed for those assets.
- Favourites are saved in your browser using localStorage.
- The budget calculator is a simple estimate for demonstration, not a live travel quote.
